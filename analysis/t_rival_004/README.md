# T-RIVAL-004: PEER-1p versus native axion-EDE n=3

Canonical decision: `PEER_1P_AIC_PREFERRED_AFTER_EXACT_SHAPE_UNION`.

ACT is excluded from the decision stack. The comparison uses one joint Planck+SPT likelihood.

The PEER amplitude was profiled over nine exact CAMB spectra. The native axion-EDE extension freed f_early, log10(zc), and theta_i. Its shape was evaluated on the union of two overlapping exact CAMB refinement grids. The winning point has exact neighbours on both sides in both shape coordinates.

- Best PEER f_early: 0.06 under all three tau priors.
- Best native axion point: f_early=0.06, log10(zc)=3.822245, theta_i=2.8915.
- Delta chi-square axion minus PEER: -0.08342 to -0.08310.
- Delta AIC axion minus PEER: +3.91658 to +3.91690.
- PEER favoured by AIC for 3/3 tau priors.

The initial extrapolated tangent optimum at much larger log10(zc) shift was explicitly falsified by exact CAMB spectra and is preserved only as a retired diagnostic.

The result is a maximum-likelihood/AIC complexity statement, not Bayesian evidence or a full global axion-EDE scan.
